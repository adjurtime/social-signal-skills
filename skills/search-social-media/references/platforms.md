# Platform routing

Use this reference to choose complementary platforms and adapt queries. Treat platform availability, search syntax, and result ranking as changeable; rely on the visible interface rather than assuming a control still exists.

## Selection matrix

| Platform | Strongest signals | Main weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xiaohongshu | Chinese lived experience, practical tutorials, configuration tips, and niche practitioner discussion | Personalized ranking, commercial content, anecdotal claims, login requirement |
| X | Breaking news, direct statements, English-language reactions, first-hand diagnostics, and rapid corrections | Rumors, bots, engagement manipulation, rapid repost chains |
| Weibo | Chinese public events, institutional accounts, public sentiment, breaking domestic news | Reposts obscure origins, trending bias, deleted or edited context |
| Reddit | English-language specialist communities, product experience, technical troubleshooting | Pseudonymous accounts, subreddit echo chambers, uneven expertise |
| LinkedIn | Companies, professional identities, hiring, industry announcements | Self-promotion, polished narratives, limited negative disclosure |
| Zhihu | Chinese technical long-form, explanation, practical guides, and rapid synthesis after a topic becomes visible | Login-sensitive search, usually secondary reporting, polished detail may exceed its evidence |
| YouTube/Bilibili | Long-form explanations, interviews, launches, demonstrations | High reading cost, weak fit for fast alerts, comments are noisy |

## Route by the basic question

Use the primary mode to choose complementary platform roles. These are defaults, not mandatory platform lists.

| Basic question | Useful first roles | Ranking emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facts and changes | X or Weibo for speed and original statements; another platform for user-observed rollout or correction | verification, event time, source proximity |
| Experience and evaluation | Xiaohongshu or Reddit for detailed use; Zhihu or video for longer context | independence, firsthand specificity, diverse positive and negative evidence |
| Methods and solutions | Reddit, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, or video according to language and demonstration needs | reproducibility, version match, failed counterexamples |
| Opinions and trends | X or Weibo for fast reactions; Reddit, Zhihu, or Xiaohongshu for community-specific interpretation | audience fit, diversity, independence, change over time |

When modes overlap, let the primary mode determine ranking and add a platform for the secondary mode only if it contributes different evidence. Do not search all platforms merely to fill the matrix.

## Default routes

- Chinese consumer experience and practical friction: Xiaohongshu + Zhihu; add Weibo only for public-event changes.
- International science, technology, markets, or policy: X + Reddit.
- Company, industry, or hiring: X + LinkedIn.
- Product and user experience: Xiaohongshu + Reddit; add video platforms when demonstrations matter.
- Breaking events: X + Weibo; distinguish social discovery from primary-source verification.
- Background explanation: Zhihu, Reddit, or video platforms as secondary sources.
- Chinese technology events: use X or another primary-source platform for confirmation; add Zhihu for Chinese explanation, practical impact, and user interpretation.

Start with two platforms. Add a third or fourth only when it contributes a different audience, geography, or evidence type.

## Xiaohongshu

- Prefer existing logged-in Chrome state.
- Generate Chinese formal names, abbreviations, community jargon, and likely misspellings.
- Add experiential aliases such as `变笨`, `降智`, `烧额度`, or `翻车` when users are likely to describe behavior rather than the formal issue.
- Compare comprehensive and recent results when available.
- Use visible filters for time, content type, or popularity only when they match the question.
- Treat screenshots of schedules, review outcomes, internal notices, or chat messages as unverified until their origin is traceable.
- Preserve technical details from tutorials and comments when they expose settings, versions, prompts, or reproducible observations, but verify claimed causes separately.
- Distinguish a current incident from older generic troubleshooting posts that reuse the same symptom words.
- Record the displayed author, relative or exact date, title, URL, and visible engagement value without guessing the engagement type.
- Exclude explicit booking, purchasing, consultation, discount, or private-message calls to action from organic experience evidence.
- Down-rank provider-heavy recommendations, interchangeable `complete guide` templates, and posts that praise one option without concrete trade-offs or negative details.
- Treat personal sequence, mistakes, changing judgments, exact conditions, and comment corrections as useful authenticity signals, not proof by themselves.

## X

- Generate formal names, abbreviations, English equivalents, hashtags, account names, and distinctive phrases.
- Use exact phrases and visible platform-supported operators only when helpful.
- Compare recent results with high-relevance results instead of relying on one ranking.
- Prefer original posts from the subject, institution, reporter, researcher, or first-hand observer over quote posts and aggregators.
- Search direct statements and English-language user reactions as separate result groups. Treat reactions as evidence of observed behavior, not as substitutes for the source's stated intent.
- Prefer user reports that include versions, settings, measured values, screenshots, or explicit reproduction steps over general sentiment.
- Preserve the timeline when a platform operator confirms a temporary experiment and later reverts it; a correction can change the current state without erasing earlier observations.
- Trace viral screenshots and copied claims back to the earliest visible post.
- Treat verified badges and follower counts as identity clues, not proof of a claim.
- Filter affiliate promotions, brand campaigns, bot amplification, coordinated slogans, and engagement farming before measuring community reaction.
- Treat repeated near-identical posts as one campaign or source cluster, not many independent opinions.

## Weibo

- Search the full entity name, common abbreviation, event term, and year.
- Prefer original posts from institutional, media, or identifiable participant accounts.
- Open repost chains when necessary to locate the original post.
- Distinguish a trending topic from evidence that the underlying claim is true.
- Separate organic discussion from brand placements, fan-club coordination, marketing accounts, and copied media text.

## Reddit

- Identify the most relevant subreddit before broadening globally.
- Compare recent posts with high-ranked discussions.
- Separate the original post from comments; comments may provide independent evidence or merely repeat the post.
- Treat moderator labels, linked documentation, reproducible details, and consistent expert history as useful context, not automatic verification.
- Check account history and subreddit norms when affiliate links, repeated recommendations, astroturfing, or coordinated promotion are plausible.

## LinkedIn

- Prefer posts from named individuals, companies, universities, and professional bodies.
- Capture the person's stated role and relationship to the topic.
- Expect announcement and self-promotion bias; look for concrete dates, documents, job records, event pages, or named counterparties.
- Use company and employee posts for their own stated position, not as independent evaluation of the company or product.

## Zhihu and video platforms

- Use Zhihu for explanations, practical guides, and synthesized impact. It may react within hours to a technology topic, but normally remains a secondary source rather than the place of first confirmation.
- Prefer logged-in native search when logged-out search is empty or visibly restricted; otherwise report the coverage gap instead of concluding that no content exists.
- Check whether an answer links the original statement or only embeds screenshots and paraphrases. Entity links, inline figures, and repeated account names are not substitutes for a direct source URL.
- Compare dates, eligibility groups, and rollout states against the primary source because explanatory posts may merge separate announcements.
- Treat technical depth and evidence quality as separate dimensions. Tables, precise numbers, confident causal explanations, and long tutorials still require traceable documentation or reproducible tests.
- Use video platforms for explanations, demonstrations, and background rather than first-line breaking-news discovery.
- Check publication or upload date and whether the content has been updated.
- Separate creator claims from source material shown or linked in the content.
- Use comments only when the question explicitly concerns audience response or comments add independently checkable information.
- Down-rank templated listicles, undeclared service-provider content, and polished explanations whose specificity exceeds their traceable evidence.
- Treat sponsorship, referral links, course sales, agency services, and repeated brand placement as commercial relationships even when the content also contains useful facts.

## Restricted and private spaces

Do not treat login-gated public posts as private merely because a platform requires an account. Conversely, do not include private messages, closed groups, paid communities, friends-only posts, or leaked personal information in ordinary social search.

If the user explicitly requests authorized private-space research, handle it as a separate, confirmation-sensitive task rather than silently expanding this skill's scope.
