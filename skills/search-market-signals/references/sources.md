# Market source routing

Use the smallest source set that can discover the signal and verify its factual core. Availability, paywalls, search interfaces, and login requirements can change; rely on the visible interface.

## Mainland China and Hong Kong

### Primary and authoritative sources

| Source | Use it for | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CNInfo | Statutory company disclosures and searchable announcements across mainland markets | Prefer the original attached filing over summaries |
| Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Beijing stock exchanges | Exchange notices, listed-company disclosures, inquiries, discipline, trading status, and listed funds | Each exchange is authoritative for its own venue and rules |
| China Securities Regulatory Commission | Regulations, enforcement, approvals, and investor warnings | Regulatory pages do not replace issuer product documents |
| Company investor-relations pages | Results, presentations, calls, and company statements | Compare material claims with statutory filings |
| Fund manager and product pages | Prospectus, periodic reports, holdings, fees, distributions, and product changes | Confirm the exact share class and effective date |
| Index providers | Methodology, constituents, classification, and rebalance notices | A theme label used by communities may not match an official index definition |

Use exchange and statutory filings as the first verification target for price-sensitive company or listed-product claims. Add regulator, issuer, fund-manager, index-provider, policy, statistical, or industry sources according to who actually controls the claimed fact.

### Investor and product communities

| Platform | Strongest signal | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xueqiu | Stock, fund, and sector theses; long-form investor discussion; cross-market aliases | Influencer incentives, product distribution, selective track records |
| Eastmoney Guba and fund communities | High-volume retail reaction, rapid rumor spread, ticker-specific slang | Very high noise, repetition, promotion, and emotional herding |
| Jisilu | ETF, LOF, QDII, convertible-bond, and product-mechanics discussion | Membership limits, niche audience, community-specific assumptions |
| Xiaohongshu | Retail fund experience, new narratives, beginner language, platform friction | Soft advertising, templated content, screenshots without provenance |
| Zhihu | Longer explanations of mechanisms, sectors, and public events | Secondary reporting, stale detail, polished claims without primary evidence |
| Weibo | Public-company events, media circulation, and short-term public attention | Repost chains, trend manipulation, origin loss |

Use Guba-like communities mainly to identify attention, emotion, rumors, and vocabulary. Never use message volume alone to confirm a market event.

## United States and international markets

### Primary and authoritative sources

| Source | Use it for | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEC EDGAR | Company filings, ownership filings, mutual-fund and ETF filings, and full-text filing search | Use the correct issuer, CIK, ticker, filing type, and filed date |
| Company investor-relations pages | Results, presentations, calls, and official releases | Prefer filed versions for material regulated disclosures |
| Relevant exchange notices | Listing status, halts, delisting, and exchange-rule events | Exchange notices do not cover all company or industry catalysts |
| ETF issuer and fund sponsor | Prospectus, holdings, fees, distributions, and product changes | Confirm timestamp and share class |
| Index providers and official data bodies | Methodology, rebalances, classifications, macro and sector series | Record release date and revision status |

### Investor communities and analytical platforms

| Platform | Strongest signal | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | Direct company or reporter statements, cashtags, rapid reactions, and corrections | Bots, impersonation, promotion, repost chains |
| Stocktwits | Ticker-centered real-time retail attention and sentiment | Hype, coordinated posting, low-context messages |
| Reddit | Subcommunity-specific theses, long discussions, and counterarguments | Anonymous expertise, echo chambers, hidden positions |
| TradingView Ideas | Published chart-based ideas, technical scenarios, and updates | Technical opinion is not factual confirmation or a forecast guarantee |
| Seeking Alpha | Long-form stock and ETF theses, news discussion, and comments | Contributor incentives, paid products, paywalls, third-party opinions |
| LinkedIn | Named executive, employee, analyst, and industry-professional statements | Corporate self-promotion and limited negative disclosure |

## Default source bundles

- Mainland company event: exchange or CNInfo + company IR + Xueqiu or Guba for interpretation.
- Mainland sector narrative: Xueqiu + Xiaohongshu or Weibo + the regulator, industry body, data release, or company filings behind the claimed catalyst.
- Fund or ETF product: fund manager or issuer + exchange and index provider + Jisilu, Xueqiu, or a relevant fund community.
- United States company event: SEC EDGAR + company IR + X, Stocktwits, Reddit, or Seeking Alpha according to the question.
- International technical narrative: official event source + TradingView for published technical scenarios + X or Stocktwits for reaction.

Start with one primary verification source and one or two community sources. Add another source only when it contributes a different fact, audience, market, or counterargument.

## Access and account handling

- Search public pages first.
- Reuse the user's logged-in Chrome session only when native results are materially better or public search is restricted.
- Ask the user to complete login, CAPTCHA, or verification manually.
- Do not request passwords, brokerage credentials, portfolio screenshots, trading access, or private paid-community content.
- Treat paywalled or inaccessible content as a coverage gap; do not reconstruct it from snippets.
